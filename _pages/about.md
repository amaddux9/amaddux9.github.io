I recently graduated from a PhD program at EPFL Lausanne, where I conducted research under the supervision of <a href='https://people.epfl.ch/maryam.kamgarpour?lang=en'>Maryam Kamgarpour</a> in the Systems Control and Multiagent Optimization Research (Sycamore) Lab. My research interests lie in the fields of game theory, mechanism design and learning in multi-agent systmes. During my PhD, I developed game-theoretic and learning-based methods for multi-agent systems with constraints and welfare objectives. Motivated by applications in transportation systems, electricity markets, ride-hailing platforms, and multi-robot systems, my research focused on equilibrium analysis and decentralized learning for constrained decision-making, as well as on mechanism design and learning dynamics to improve social welfare.

Prior to my PhD, I obtained a MSc. in Statistics from ETH Zürich (2021), where I did my master's thesis at the Automatic Control Laboratory (Ifa) under the supervision of <a href='https://people.ee.ethz.ch/~floriand/'>Florian Dörfler</a> and <a href='https://people.math.ethz.ch/~fadouab/'>Fadoua Balabdaoui</a>. I, furthermore, obtained a BSc. in Mathematics (2019) and a BSc. in Economics (2018) from the Albert-Ludwigs-University Freiburg. In the past, I interned at Daimler Benz and the German federal parliament. Throughout my undergraduate and graduate studies I have been a fellow of the Friedrich Nauman Foundation. I was also a fellow of the Accenture female talent program.
